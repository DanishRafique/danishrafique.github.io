Rizq — Islamic finance

Your money. Your obligations. One ledger.

Rizq is built to answer five questions: what money you have, what part of it is actually yours, what part carries an Islamic obligation, where it came from and where it went — with the method behind every figure shown on screen.

A zakat engine, not a calculator

Mark which accounts and assets are zakatable, set your nisab basis and madhhab — Hanafi, Shafi’i, Maliki or Hanbali — and Rizq assesses your wealth against the threshold across the hawl. Every assessment is stored with the inputs that produced it, so last year’s figure never silently changes when a price does.

Riba tracked, separated, purified

Flag an account as interest-bearing and the interest it credits is marked as riba rather than counted as income — on one-off entries and on recurring rules alike. The purification view totals what has accrued so you can give it away, and keeps a record of what you already have.

Sadaqah & the eight asnaf

Log charity as it happens and record which of the eight categories named in Surah At-Tawbah 9:60 each zakat payment went to. One payment, one category — nothing is apportioned on your behalf.

The Hijri calendar, everywhere

Switch periods from Gregorian to Hijri and months, reports and totals follow. Ramadan budgets, a Hajj or Umrah savings goal with a target Hijri year, and Qurbani and Eid presets all sit on the same period system.

Spending screening that states, never scores

Mark a category Clear, Review or Avoid and Rizq summarises your spending against your own markings. Nothing is classified for you, there is no halal score, and no line of it grades your life.

Islamic financing & debt

Record a debt as qard hasan, murabaha or ijara — agreed total and what is left, with no interest-rate field anywhere, because these contracts have none. Takaful contributions ride the ordinary recurring-expense path.

A statement you can hand over

Export a zakat statement as a PDF for one assessment or a whole year — restating the figures exactly as they were frozen, never recomputed at export time.

Rizq states the facts. It does not issue a fatwa.

Every Islamic figure in the app shows the rule that produced it and the inputs you gave, and the wording stays descriptive throughout — “review” and “avoid”, never a ruling. Madhhabs differ, and Rizq names which position it applied rather than pretending there is only one. For a binding opinion on your own situation, ask a qualified scholar — Rizq keeps the records that make that conversation easy.

Privacy

There is no Vynci server holding your money data

Both apps are private ledgers, not banking apps. They do not connect to a bank, cannot read your statements and cannot move money. Every figure on screen is calculated from entries you typed yourself.

01

Local first

Your transactions, balances and receipts live in a database on your phone. We run nothing that collects them.

02

No tracking

No analytics SDKs, no advertising, no behavioural profiling, in any product we ship.

03

Portable

Everything you enter can be exported to CSV or PDF and taken elsewhere. No lock-in.

04

Plainly said

Each app states what it collects and why, in words rather than boilerplate.

Why the apps ask for a Google account

Two Google services are used, and only these two. Google Sign-In identifies which account a backup belongs to, and lets the app notice when a different person signs in on a shared device. Google Drive stores your backup — a compressed copy of the app database and your receipt images — inside your own Google account.

The apps request the drive.appdata scope only: Drive’s hidden, application-specific folder. They cannot see, read, list or modify any other file in your Drive, and we have no access to the backup at all. It exists so that replacing or resetting your phone does not cost you your records. You can revoke it any time from your Google Account permissions page.

How we build

Small apps, built carefully

  • One codebase, two apps. Rizq is built from the same source as Vynance, so a fix in the ledger reaches both on the same day.
  • One database format. Both apps read and write the same schema, which is why a local backup moves between them without a migration.
  • Native Android. Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, no web view, no cross-platform wrapper — Material 3, dark theme, dynamic colour and home-screen widgets.
  • Nothing invented. The apps do not guess prices, estimate what you did not enter, or fill a gap with a plausible number.
  • Tested where it counts. The money rules and the Islamic rules are pure, testable logic with their own unit tests, kept apart from the screens.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do the apps connect to my bank?

No. Neither app links to a bank, imports statements or has any ability to move money. You enter transactions yourself, or set up recurring rules for the ones that repeat.

Can I use them without signing in to Google?

Sign-in exists so a backup has an owner and a shared device does not show one person’s records to another. The ledger itself works entirely offline — no network connection is needed to record, budget or report.

Which madhhab does Rizq follow for zakat?

Whichever you choose. Hanafi, Shafi’i, Maliki and Hanbali positions are configurable, and the methodology view names the rule that produced the figure you are looking at. Shia khums is not currently supported.

Is Rizq issuing religious rulings?

No. Rizq states financial facts, shows its method and preserves your inputs. Anything that would amount to a ruling — classifying your spending for you, scoring it, or calculating an inheritance share — is deliberately not built.

I already use Vynance. Can I move to Rizq?

Yes. Export a local backup from Vynance and restore it in Rizq — the database format is identical, so nothing is lost and no conversion runs. Google Drive backups are scoped per app, so use the local file for the move.

What does it cost?

Both apps are free to install, with the everyday ledger, budgets, debts and the zakat features included. A premium subscription unlocks the heavier export and reporting tools. There are no ads in either app, at any tier.
