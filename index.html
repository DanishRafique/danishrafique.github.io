How we build

Small apps, built carefully

We are a small studio, and that shapes what we make: no growth machinery, no clever tricks, and no feature we could not explain in one honest sentence.

01

Local first

What you enter stays on your phone. We keep nothing, and we could not read it if we wanted to.

02

No tracking

Nothing watches how you use our apps. No advertising, no profiling, ever.

03

Your data is portable

Everything you enter can be taken with you and opened elsewhere. Leaving is always an option.

04

Plainly explained

Every app says what it handles and why, in words anyone can read.

  • Made for the phone you have. Our apps look and behave like the rest of your phone, follow your light or dark setting, and put what you need on the home screen.
  • Nothing invented. Our apps do not guess prices, estimate what you did not enter, or fill a gap with a plausible number.
  • Checked before it ships. Every figure an app shows is checked against worked examples before a release goes out.
  • Fewer apps, better kept. We would rather keep two apps correct than launch a third.

Privacy

There is no Vynci server holding your data

Our apps are private record-keepers, not banking apps. They do not connect to a bank, do not read your statements and cannot move money. Every figure on screen is calculated from entries you typed yourself, on your own device.

Where an app offers a backup, it goes into a private corner of your own Google Drive that only that app can use. It cannot see anything else in your Drive, and we cannot reach the backup at all. Each app’s own policy sets out exactly what it handles.
