  • Your entries: the app does not connect to your bank and cannot verify anything. Balances, totals and zakat bases are only as accurate as what you type.
  • Gold and silver prices are yours. The app fetches no metal prices and ships no price feed. Nisab thresholds and zakat figures are computed from rates you enter, at the moment you enter them.
  • Exchange rates come from the public Frankfurter reference API, are typically updated daily, and are indicative only. They are not live market rates, do not include spreads or fees, and must not be relied on for actual currency conversion or trading.
