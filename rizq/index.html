What Rizq adds

Your money. Your obligations. One ledger.

Rizq is built to answer five questions: what money you have, what part of it is actually yours, what part carries an Islamic obligation, where it came from and where it went. Islamic finance sits in the core of the ledger rather than in a calculator bolted onto the side.

A zakat engine, not a calculator

Mark which accounts and assets are zakatable, record gold and silver by weight, add receivables and business inventory, then set your nisab basis and madhhab — Hanafi, Shafi’i, Maliki or Hanbali. Rizq assesses your wealth against the threshold across the hawl and stores each assessment with the exact inputs that produced it, so last year’s figure never silently changes when a price does.

Riba tracked, separated, purified

Flag an account as interest-bearing and the interest it credits is marked as riba rather than counted as income — on one-off entries and on recurring rules alike, so a standing monthly credit cannot slip into your income totals unnoticed. The purification view totals what has accrued and keeps a record of what you have already given away.

Zakat al-Fitr

Per household head, by your chosen staple and its sa’ weight, with the madhhab position named beside the figure — and a reminder in the last third of Ramadan, because the timing is the point.

Sadaqah & the eight asnaf

Log charity as it happens and record which of the eight categories named in Surah At-Tawbah 9:60 each zakat payment went to. One payment, one category — nothing is apportioned on your behalf.

The Hijri calendar, everywhere

Switch periods from Gregorian to Hijri and months, reports and totals follow, with a sighting offset you control. Ramadan budgets, a Hajj or Umrah goal with a target Hijri year, and Qurbani and Eid presets all sit on that same period system.

Spending screening that states, never scores

Mark a category Clear, Review or Avoid and Rizq summarises your spending against your own markings. Nothing is classified for you, there is no halal score, and no line of it grades your life.

Islamic financing & debt

Record a debt as qard hasan, murabaha or ijara — the agreed total and what is left, with no interest-rate field anywhere, because these contracts have none. Takaful contributions ride the ordinary recurring-expense path.

A statement you can hand over

Export a zakat statement as a PDF for one assessment or a whole year — restating the figures exactly as they were frozen, never recomputed at export time.

Rizq states the facts. It does not issue a fatwa.

Every Islamic figure shows the rule that produced it and the inputs you gave, and the wording stays descriptive throughout — “review” and “avoid”, never a ruling. Madhhabs differ, and Rizq names which position it applied rather than pretending there is only one. For a binding opinion on your own situation, ask a qualified scholar — Rizq keeps the records that make that conversation short.

And everything else

A complete household ledger underneath

Rizq is the Islamic edition of Vynance, built from the same source. Nothing is traded away for the Islamic features — the everyday app is all there.

Transactions, accounts & budgets

Expenses, income and transfers across bank, cash, wallet, credit-card and investment accounts, each in its own currency; category budgets with local alerts; recurring rules for the entries that repeat.

Reports, debts, investments & goals

Category breakdowns, trends, cash-flow and savings charts; money lent and borrowed per person; holdings, pensions and savings goals recorded from your provider’s statements.

Receipts, voice entry, widgets and export

Attach a receipt photo, add an entry by speaking it, check your balance from a home-screen widget or the quick-settings tile, and export the whole ledger to CSV or a formatted PDF whenever you want it elsewhere.

Our stance

What Rizq will not do

Some things are left out on purpose. These are decisions, not gaps.

01

No halal score

No single number summarising your spending against a religious standard. That would be the app grading a life.

02

No inheritance calculator

Faraid is a legal instrument acted on at the worst possible moment. It needs a scholar, not a budgeting app.

03

No guessed prices

Gold and silver rates are the ones you enter. The app ships no price feed and invents no figure you did not state.

04

No ruling by default

No category arrives pre-classified as permitted or not. Your markings, your madhhab, your call.

Privacy

Your records — financial and religious — stay on your device

Rizq stores every transaction, balance, receipt, zakat assessment, charity record and madhhab preference in a private database on your phone. There is no Rizq server. We do not receive, store, sell or analyse any of it, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.

Your religious observance is not data we collect. It never leaves the device except inside a backup you choose to create, in your own Google Drive app folder that we cannot browse.

The app requests the drive.appdata scope only — Drive’s hidden, application-specific folder. It cannot see, read, list or modify any other file in your Drive. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Questions

Frequently asked

Which madhhab does the zakat engine follow?

Whichever you choose. Hanafi, Shafi’i, Maliki and Hanbali positions are configurable, and the methodology view names the rule that produced the figure you are looking at. Shia khums is not currently supported.

Where do the gold and silver prices come from?

From you. Rizq ships no price feed, because no source it could reach without an API key would be reliable enough to base an obligation on. You enter the rate you are using, and the assessment records it alongside the figure so you can see later what it was based on.

Is Rizq issuing religious rulings?

No. Rizq states financial facts, shows its method and preserves your inputs. Anything that would amount to a ruling — classifying your spending for you, scoring it, or calculating an inheritance share — is deliberately not built. Consult a qualified scholar for a binding opinion.

Can I move over from Vynance?

Yes. Export a local backup from Vynance and restore it in Rizq — the database format is identical, so nothing is lost and no conversion runs. Google Drive backups are scoped per app, so use the local file for the move.

Does the Hijri calendar need a network connection?

No. Hijri dates are computed on your device from tabular data shipped inside the app, with a sighting offset you can set yourself. Nothing about your dates or observance is requested from a server.

What does it cost?

Rizq is free to install. Zakat on wealth, Zakat al-Fitr, riba tracking, sadaqah and asnaf records, the screening summary, Islamic financing types and the seasonal budgets and goals are all in the free tier. A premium subscription raises the account and budget limits and unlocks the heavier reporting and export tools, including the zakat statement PDF. No ads at any tier.
