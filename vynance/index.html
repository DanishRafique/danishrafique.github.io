What Vynance does

Vynance is a personal finance record-keeping app for Android phones. You enter your income and expenses; the app organises them into accounts, categories and budgets, and shows you where your money actually goes.

It is a private ledger, not a banking app. Vynance does not connect to any bank, does not read your statements and cannot move money. Every figure it shows is calculated from entries you typed yourself.

Transactions & accounts

Log expenses, income and transfers across multiple accounts — bank, cash, wallet, credit card or investment — each in its own currency.

Budgets & alerts

Set monthly limits per category and get a local notification as you approach them.

Reports & insights

Category breakdowns, spending trends, cash-flow and savings charts, and plain-language summaries of what changed month to month.

Debts & repayments

Track money lent and borrowed per person, record part-payments, and see what is outstanding and what is overdue.

Investments & pensions

Record the value of holdings and retirement plans over time from your provider's statements, and see contributions against growth.

Receipts, voice & widgets

Attach a receipt photo, add a transaction by speaking it, or check your balance from a home-screen widget.

Your data stays on your device

Vynance stores every transaction, balance and receipt in a private database on your phone. There is no Vynance server. We do not receive, store, sell or analyse your financial records, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.

The only copy that ever leaves your phone is a backup you choose to create.

Why Vynance asks for a Google account

Vynance uses two Google services, and only these two:

  • Google Sign-In identifies which account a backup belongs to, and lets the app detect when a different person signs in on a shared device so one user's records are never shown to another.
  • Google Drive (app data folder) stores your backup — a compressed copy of the app database and your receipt images — inside your own Google account.

The app requests the drive.appdata scope only. That is Drive's hidden, application-specific folder: Vynance cannot see, read, list or modify any other file in your Drive, and the developer has no access to the backup at all. It exists so that replacing or resetting your phone does not cost you your records.

You can revoke this access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
