How we build

  • Local first. Your data lives on your device. We run no server that collects it.
  • No tracking. No analytics SDKs, no advertising, no behavioural profiling in any product we ship.
  • Your data is portable. Everything you enter can be exported and taken elsewhere.
  • Plainly explained. Every product says what it collects and why, in words rather than boilerplate.
